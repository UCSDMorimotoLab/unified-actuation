A Unified Continuum Robot Actuation Platform

Open-source hardware and software to help researchers get started with high-DOF robots. We focus on continuum robots, but the platform is agnostic to robot type!

Yunti Xu Aedan Mangan Miheer Potdar Alex Lu Tania K. Morimoto
arXivComing soon Hardware Software
  • Modular
  • Scales to many DOFs
  • ROS-based
  • Tested with TDCRs, CTRs, and CPPRs
  • Mounts on a dVRK, robot arm, or linear stage

Sub-millimeter translational accuracy and less than 5° rotational error, over ranges of 30 mm and 180°, respectively.
